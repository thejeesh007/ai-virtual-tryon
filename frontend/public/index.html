3. Physics drape (preview)

Real cloth simulation (stretch + bend + body collision), not the measurement heatmap above. Runs a genuine physics simulation per request -- expect roughly 20s-1min, not seconds. "Generic fabric" garments aren't a real product -- they're built from a standard body + fabric-ease table, and the very first request for a given fabric/size builds its pattern on the fly (adds up to another ~30s just that first time).

Two separate signals below, on purpose: the color on the garment is relative drape tightness -- which part of THIS garment sits closer to (red) or further from (blue) the body than the rest of it. Any top's chest/shoulders read tighter than its own hem just from how it hangs, at any size -- so this is NOT a good/bad verdict, just where the fabric sits closer/looser on itself. The "Fit: Snug / Good fit / Loose" text after draping is the actual size verdict, based on the real average gap to your body.
